Questions league directors ask

Do my players have to download anything?

No. Everyone keeps scoring in UDisc like they do now. Only you open Tag Night, after the round, with the exported leaderboard.

How do I get the file out of UDisc?

In your league or event: Event tools → Leaderboard → Actions → Export. Upload that file in step 1. If your phone won’t open it here, open it in Sheets or Excel, select the whole table, copy, and paste it into the Paste tab.

UDisc says everyone is unpaid. Does that matter?

No. Most leagues take cash at the tee and never tick “paid” in UDisc, so Tag Night counts everyone as paid. Untick anyone who didn’t pay in the table.

Who plays for tags?

Anyone on your tag list who finished tonight. If someone left their tag at home, untick “Playing for a tag” next to their name and their tag stays put. The best score among tag players takes the lowest tag in play, and so on down. Tags of people who didn’t play never move.

What about casual rounds where two people swap tags?

Use the Type tab: enter just those players with their scores and tags (like “Ray 50 #1”), then lock it in. It updates the same tag list.

How are ties handled?

For tags, you pick: the lower tag coming in wins, or you play it off and set the order. For money, tied players share the payouts of the places they tie for, like PDGA events do.

Where is my data? What if I lose it?

On this phone or computer only. Nothing is uploaded. Some phones clear website data after a week without a visit, so after each lock-in save the backup file (free) or copy the tag list. The players’ link carries tonight’s results and the tag list inside the link itself.
